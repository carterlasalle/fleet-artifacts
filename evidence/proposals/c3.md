# C3 — The Page That Admits Itself

lens: lateral provocation (de Bono) — "state an absurd premise, mine it for the one usable idea"
vehicle: document

## The premise
A page whose entire content is the list of things its own author could not prove.
Absurd as a product; mined for one usable idea: every fleet artifact already carries
an "Honest limits" section, and those sections are the fleet's real backlog — the only
place it says out loud what it does not know. Nothing collects them.

## The one usable idea
`unproven-<date>.html`: every limits/caveat/unresolved passage across the fleet's shipped
pages, with its artifact path and date, ranked oldest first, and one line at the top
naming how many of them a later artifact already closed.
